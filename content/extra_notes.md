# Eksik konular için kısa notlar
# Biçim: "## KİMLİK Başlık" ve altında mini-markdown.

## A1.G01 to be (am / is / are)
| Özne | Olumlu | Olumsuz | Soru |
|---|---|---|---|
| I | I **am** | I**'m not** | **Am** I...? |
| he / she / it | she **is** | she **isn't** | **Is** she...? |
| you / we / they | they **are** | they **aren't** | **Are** they...? |

- Kısa cevap: Are you tired? → Yes, I **am**. / No, I**'m not**.
- ⚠️ Kısa olumlu cevapta kısaltma yok: Yes, I'm ❌ → Yes, I **am** ✅
- to be varsa do/does gelmez: Are you happy? ✅ / Do you happy? ❌

## A1.G02 this / that / these / those
| | Yakın | Uzak |
|---|---|---|
| Tekil | **this** book | **that** book |
| Çoğul | **these** books | **those** books |

- This **is** my pen. / These **are** my pens.
- Telefonda kendini tanıtma: **This is** Ali. (Ben Ali.)
- ⚠️ these/those + çoğul isim + **are**: these book ❌ → these **books** ✅

## A1.G03 Düzensiz çoğullar
| Tekil | Çoğul |
|---|---|
| man / woman | **men / women** |
| child | **children** |
| person | **people** |
| tooth / foot | **teeth / feet** |
| mouse | **mice** |
| fish / sheep | **fish / sheep** (değişmez) |

- ⚠️ people zaten çoğul: people **are** ✅ / peoples ❌ (halklar anlamı hariç)
- ⚠️ -f / -fe → -ves: knife → knives, wife → wives, leaf → leaves

## A1.G04 can / can't
- **can + yalın fiil** = -ebilir (yetenek, izin, rica)
- Tüm öznelerde aynı: He **can swim**. (cans ❌, can swims ❌)
- Olumsuz: **can't** (cannot). Soru: **Can** you drive? → Yes, I **can**. / No, I **can't**.
- İzin: **Can** I open the window?

## A1.G05 have got / has got
| Özne | Olumlu | Olumsuz | Soru |
|---|---|---|---|
| I / you / we / they | have got ('ve got) | haven't got | **Have** you got...? |
| he / she / it | has got ('s got) | hasn't got | **Has** she got...? |

- Anlam = have (sahip olmak): I've got a car = I have a car.
- Kısa cevap: Have you got a pen? → Yes, I **have**. / No, I **haven't**.
- ⚠️ have got ile do kullanılmaz: Do you have got ❌. Ya **Do you have** ya **Have you got**.
- ⚠️ Geçmişte have got yok: I **had** a car.

## A1.G06 Sayılabilen / sayılamayan + some / any
- Sayılabilen: a book, two **books** (tekil/çoğul olur)
- Sayılamayan: water, money, rice, bread, milk, advice, information (çoğul -s almaz, a/an almaz)

| | Olumlu | Olumsuz | Soru |
|---|---|---|---|
| Çoğul / sayılamayan | **some** | **any** | **any** |

- There is **some** milk. / There isn't **any** milk. / Is there **any** milk?
- ⚠️ Teklif ve ricada soruda da **some**: Would you like **some** tea? / Can I have **some** water?

## A1.G07 How much / How many
- **How many** + çoğul sayılabilen: How many **books** have you got?
- **How much** + sayılamayan: How much **water** do you drink?
- Fiyat sormak: **How much is** this shirt? / **How much are** these shoes?
- ⚠️ How many money ❌ → How **much** money ✅

## A1.G08 Yer edatları
| Edat | Anlam | Örnek |
|---|---|---|
| in | içinde | in the box, in the kitchen |
| on | üstünde (temas) | on the table, on the wall |
| under | altında | under the bed |
| next to | yanında | next to the bank |
| behind / in front of | arkasında / önünde | behind the door |
| between | arasında | between the bank and the cafe |
| opposite | karşısında | opposite the school |

- Şehir, ülke → **in**: in Ankara. Adres/nokta → **at**: at the bus stop, at home, at school.

## A1.G09 like / love / hate + -ing
- Hoşlanma fiillerinden sonra fiil **-ing**: I **like reading**. She **loves cooking**. They **hate waiting**.
- don't like / don't mind (umursamam) + -ing: I **don't mind walking**.
- he/she → like**s**: He **likes swimming**. (He like ❌)
- ⚠️ **would like + to**: I'd like **to** go. (bkz. A1 konu 12)

## A1.G10 be going to (plan ve kanıta dayalı tahmin)
- Yapı: **am/is/are + going to + yalın fiil**
- Önceden yapılmış plan: I**'m going to visit** my aunt this weekend.
- Görünen bir işarete dayalı tahmin: Look at the clouds! It**'s going to rain**.
- Olumsuz: I'm **not** going to... Soru: **Are** you going to...?
- ⚠️ is going to **goes** ❌ → is going to **go** ✅

## A1.G11 Sayılar, tarih ve saat
- Sıra sayıları: first, second, third, fourth, fifth, ... twelfth, twentieth, twenty-first
- Tarih: **on** 5 May → "the fifth of May" / "May fifth". Yıl: 2024 → "twenty twenty-four".
- Saat: 7:00 seven o'clock · 7:15 **quarter past** seven · 7:30 **half past** seven · 7:45 **quarter to** eight · 7:10 ten **past** seven · 7:50 ten **to** eight
- What time is it? → It's half past three.
- ⚠️ 13 thirteen ≠ 30 thirty (vurgu: thir**TEEN** / **THIR**ty)

## A1.G12 and / but / or / because / so
| Bağlaç | Anlam | Örnek |
|---|---|---|
| and | ve | I like tea **and** coffee. |
| but | ama | It's cheap **but** good. |
| or | veya | Tea **or** coffee? |
| because | çünkü (sebep) | I stayed home **because** I was ill. |
| so | bu yüzden (sonuç) | I was ill, **so** I stayed home. |

- ⚠️ because sebebin önüne, so sonucun önüne gelir.

## A1.G13 Sıfatların yeri
- Sıfat **isimden önce** gelir: a **big** house (a house big ❌)
- ya da **be fiilinden sonra**: The house **is big**.
- Sıfat çoğul almaz: two **big** houses (bigs ❌)
- a/an sıfata göre seçilir: **an** old car, **a** new car

## A1.G14 Wh- soruları (to be ile)
- Yapı: **Soru kelimesi + am/is/are + özne?**
- Where **is** the station? / How old **are** you? / What **is** your name? / Who **is** that man?
- How **are** you? → I'm fine. / What's the weather like? → It's sunny.
- ⚠️ to be sorusunda do yok: Where **is** she? (Where does she is ❌)

## A2.G37 will vs be going to
| | will | be going to |
|---|---|---|
| Karar | **o anda** verilen karar: The phone is ringing. I**'ll** answer it. | **önceden** verilmiş plan: I**'m going to** buy a car next month. |
| Tahmin | görüşe dayalı: I think she**'ll** win. | göz önündeki kanıta dayalı: Look! He**'s going to** fall. |
| Diğer | söz, teklif, rica: I'll help you. / Will you open the door? | |

- Kesin randevu/ayarlama: Present Continuous: I**'m meeting** Ali at 5.

## A2.G38 Present Simple vs Present Continuous
| Present Simple | Present Continuous |
|---|---|
| alışkanlık, rutin, genel doğru | şu an olan, geçici durum |
| I **work** in a bank. | I**'m working** from home this week. |
| every day, usually, often | now, at the moment, today, this week |

- Durum fiilleri (know, want, like, need...) continuous olmaz: I **know** ✅ / I'm knowing ❌
- Soru: What **do** you do? (meslek) / What **are** you doing? (şu an)

## A2.G39 much / many / a lot of / a few / a little
| | Sayılabilen (çoğul) | Sayılamayan |
|---|---|---|
| çok | many / a lot of | much / a lot of |
| biraz | a few | a little |

- much / many genelde **olumsuz ve soruda**: I don't have **much** time. / Are there **many** people?
- Olumluda **a lot of** daha doğal: I have **a lot of** friends.
- a few **books** / a little **money**

## A2.G40 Fiil + -ing / fiil + to
- **+ -ing**: enjoy, finish, mind, avoid, keep, stop, like, love, hate, keen on, good at (edattan sonra daima -ing)
- **+ to**: want, need, decide, hope, plan, learn, would like, try, forget, promise
- I **enjoy swimming**. / I **want to swim**. / I **decided to stay**.
- ⚠️ Edattan sonra fiil -ing: interested **in learning**, good **at cooking**

## A2.G41 Edilgen yapı (Present / Past Simple)
- Yapı: **am/is/are + V3** (geniş), **was/were + V3** (geçmiş)
- English **is spoken** here. / This bridge **was built** in 1973.
- Yapan önemliyse **by**: The book **was written by** Orhan Pamuk.
- Olumsuz: isn't / wasn't + V3. Soru: **Is** it made...? / **Was** it built...?
- ⚠️ V3 şart: was build ❌ → was **built** ✅

## A2.G42 who / which / that (relative clause)
- **who** → insan: The man **who** lives next door is a doctor.
- **which** → nesne, hayvan: The car **which** I bought is red.
- **that** → ikisi için de: the man **that** / the car **that**
- **where** → yer: the city **where** I was born
- ⚠️ Özne tekrar edilmez: The man who **he** lives ❌ → The man who lives ✅

## A2.G43 a / the / article yok
- Meslekte **a/an**: She's **a** doctor.
- Tekrar edilen, bilinen → **the**: I saw a film. **The** film was great.
- Genel anlam, çoğul/sayılamayan → article yok: **Dogs** are friendly. **Money** isn't everything.
- Article yok: go to **school / work / bed**, at **home**, by **bus**, öğünler (have **breakfast**), sporlar (play **football**)
- **the** + çalgılar (play **the** guitar), nehir/deniz (**the** Black Sea), tek olanlar (**the** sun)

## A2.G44 ago / during / until / by
- **ago** = önce (geçmiş zamanla): two days **ago**, a long time **ago**
- **during** + isim = sırasında: **during** the film, **during** the summer
- **until** = -e kadar (süre devam eder): I'll wait **until** 6.
- **by** = en geç: Finish it **by** Friday. (Cumaya kadar bitir, Cuma en son)
- ⚠️ during + cümle olmaz → **while**: while I was eating

## A2.G45 Sık phrasal verb'ler
| Fiil | Anlam |
|---|---|
| get up | kalkmak |
| wake up | uyanmak |
| look for | aramak |
| look after | bakmak (çocuk, hayvan) |
| turn on / off | açmak / kapatmak |
| put on / take off | giymek / çıkarmak |
| give up | vazgeçmek, bırakmak |
| find out | öğrenmek, bulmak |
| pick up | almak, kaldırmak |
| go out | dışarı çıkmak |
| come back | geri dönmek |

- Nesne zamir ise araya girer: turn **it** on ✅ / turn on it ❌

## A2.G46 Hareket edatları
| Edat | Anlam | Örnek |
|---|---|---|
| into / out of | içine / dışına | She went **into** the room. |
| across | karşıdan karşıya | walk **across** the street |
| through | içinden geçerek | drive **through** the tunnel |
| along | boyunca | walk **along** the river |
| up / down | yukarı / aşağı | go **up** the stairs |
| over / under | üzerinden / altından | jump **over** the wall |
| past | önünden geçerek | walk **past** the bank |
| towards | -e doğru | run **towards** the door |

## A2.G47 So do I / Neither do I
- Olumlu cümleye katılma: **So + yardımcı fiil + I**
  - I like tea. → **So do I.** / I'm tired. → **So am I.** / I can swim. → **So can I.**
- Olumsuz cümleye katılma: **Neither + yardımcı fiil + I**
  - I don't like tea. → **Neither do I.** / I wasn't there. → **Neither was I.**
- Gündelik: Me too. (olumlu) / Me neither. (olumsuz)
- Yardımcı fiil, but yankısındaki (A2 konu 22) mantıkla seçilir.

## B1.G01 Present Simple vs Continuous (geçici ve değişen durumlar)
- Geçici durum: I**'m living** with my parents **at the moment** (kalıcı değil).
- Değişen/gelişen durum: It**'s getting** colder. / Prices **are going up**.
- **always + Continuous** = şikâyet: He**'s always losing** his keys!
- Kalıcı durum ve rutin: I **live** in Ankara. / I **get up** at 7.

## B1.G02 Gelecek biçimleri
| Biçim | Ne zaman | Örnek |
|---|---|---|
| Present Continuous | kesin ayarlama, randevu | I**'m meeting** Ali tomorrow. |
| be going to | niyet, plan; kanıta dayalı tahmin | I**'m going to** learn German. |
| will | anlık karar, söz, görüş | I**'ll** call you later. |
| Present Simple | tarife, program | The train **leaves** at 6. |

## B1.G03 Present Perfect Continuous
- Yapı: **have/has been + -ing**
- Geçmişte başlayıp hâlâ süren (süre vurgusu): I**'ve been waiting** for an hour.
- Yeni bitmiş, izi görünen: You're wet. **Have** you **been running**?
- **How long have you been** learning English? → For two years. / Since 2024.

## B1.G04 Present Perfect Simple vs Continuous
- Simple → sonuç / miktar: I**'ve read** three books this month.
- Continuous → süreç / süre: I**'ve been reading** all day.
- Durum fiilleri continuous olmaz: I**'ve known** him for years. (I've been knowing ❌)

## B1.G06 Anlatı zamanları
- Arka plan → Past Continuous: It **was raining**...
- Ana olaylar → Past Simple: ...when I **arrived**.
- Daha önce olan → Past Perfect: Everyone **had left**.
- When I **arrived**, it **was raining** and everyone **had** already **left**.

## B1.G07 used to vs would
- **used to** → geçmiş alışkanlık + geçmiş durum: I **used to have** long hair.
- **would** → sadece geçmişte tekrarlanan eylem: Every summer we **would go** to the village.
- ⚠️ Durum fiilinde would olmaz: I would have long hair ❌ → I **used to** have long hair ✅

## B1.G08 be used to / get used to
- **be used to + -ing / isim** = alışkın olmak: I**'m used to getting** up early.
- **get used to + -ing** = alışmak (süreç): I**'m getting used to living** alone.
- ⚠️ used to + V1 (eskiden yapardım) ile karıştırma: I used to **work** ≠ I'm used to **working**.

## B1.G09 Future Continuous
- Yapı: **will be + -ing** → gelecekte belli bir anda sürüyor olacak
- This time tomorrow I**'ll be flying** to London.
- At 8 pm I**'ll be watching** the match, so don't call.

## B1.G10 Past Perfect Continuous
- Yapı: **had been + -ing** → geçmişteki bir andan önce bir süredir devam eden
- I **had been working** for five hours when he called.
- She was tired because she **had been running**.

## B1.G11 Çıkarım: must / might / can't
- **must** = eminim öyle (%95): He's been working all day. He **must be** tired.
- **might / may / could** = olabilir (%50): She **might be** at home.
- **can't** = eminim öyle değil: That **can't be** Ali. He's in London.
- ⚠️ Bu anlamda mustn't kullanılmaz: It mustn't be true ❌ → It **can't** be true ✅

## B1.G12 should have / could have / might have + V3
- **should have + V3** = yapmalıydın (yapmadın, pişmanlık): You **should have told** me.
- **shouldn't have + V3**: I **shouldn't have eaten** so much.
- **could have + V3** = yapabilirdin (ama yapmadın): You **could have called** me.
- **might / must / can't have + V3** = geçmiş hakkında çıkarım: He **must have forgotten**. / She **can't have seen** us.

## B1.G13 had better / ought to
- **had better (’d better) + V1** = yapsan iyi olur (yoksa kötü sonuç): You**'d better hurry** or you'll miss the bus.
- Olumsuz: You**'d better not** be late.
- **ought to + V1** = should ile aynı (tavsiye): You **ought to** see a doctor.
- ⚠️ had better to ❌ / had better going ❌ → had better **go** ✅

## B1.G14 need to / needn't / be allowed to
- **need to** = gerekmek: I **need to** buy milk. Olumsuz: **don't need to** / **needn't** = gerek yok.
- **be allowed to** = izinli olmak: We**'re not allowed to** park here. / **Were** you **allowed to** go out?
- can (izin) ile benzer ama geçmiş ve gelecekte daha çok be allowed to kullanılır.

## B1.G17 unless / as long as / in case
- **unless** = if ... not: **Unless** you hurry, you'll be late.
- **as long as** = ...dığı sürece: You can borrow it **as long as** you bring it back.
- **in case** = ...ihtimaline karşı: Take an umbrella **in case** it rains.
- ⚠️ Hepsinden sonra **will yok**: in case it **rains** (it will rain ❌)

## B1.G18 wish / if only
- Şimdiki duruma dilek → **wish + Past Simple**: I **wish** I **had** more time. / I wish I **were** taller.
- Geçmişe pişmanlık → **wish + Past Perfect**: I **wish** I **had studied** harder.
- Başkasının davranışından şikâyet → **wish + would**: I wish you **would** stop talking.
- if only = wish'in daha güçlü hâli.

## B1.G19 Passive (Present / Past Simple)
- Yapı: **be + V3**: Cars **are made** in this factory. / The house **was built** in 1920.
- Yapanı söylemek gerekirse **by**: It was painted **by** Picasso.
- Ne zaman: yapan bilinmiyor, önemsiz ya da belli: My bike **was stolen**.

## B1.G20 Passive (diğer zamanlar)
| Zaman | Yapı | Örnek |
|---|---|---|
| Present Continuous | am/is/are being + V3 | The road **is being repaired**. |
| Present Perfect | have/has been + V3 | The car **has been sold**. |
| Past Perfect | had been + V3 | The tickets **had been sold**. |
| will | will be + V3 | It **will be finished** tomorrow. |
| Modal | modal + be + V3 | It **must be done** today. / It **can be seen**. |

## B1.G21 have something done
- Yapı: **have + nesne + V3** → işi başkasına yaptırmak
- I **had my hair cut**. (Saçımı kestirdim.) / We**'re having** the house **painted**.
- get something done = daha gündelik: I need to **get** my car **fixed**.

## B1.G22 Reported speech (ifadeler)
| Doğrudan | Dolaylı |
|---|---|
| am/is → was | "I'm tired." → He said (that) he **was** tired. |
| Present Simple → Past Simple | "I live here." → She said she **lived** there. |
| Past Simple / Present Perfect → Past Perfect | "I saw it." → He said he **had seen** it. |
| will → would, can → could | "I'll help." → She said she **would** help. |

- **say** nesnesiz: He said that... / **tell** + kişi: He **told me** that...
- Yer/zaman da kayar: here → there, today → that day, tomorrow → the next day

## B1.G23 Reported questions
- Evet/hayır sorusu → **if / whether**: "Are you OK?" → She asked **if I was** OK.
- Wh- sorusu → soru kelimesi + **düz cümle sırası**: "Where do you live?" → He asked where **I lived**.
- ⚠️ do/does/did kalkar, soru sırası olmaz: He asked where did I live ❌

## B1.G24 Reported commands / requests
- tell / ask + kişi + **to + V1**: "Wait!" → He **told me to wait**.
- Olumsuz: **not to**: "Don't go." → She **asked me not to go**.

## B1.G25 Defining relative clauses
| Zamir | Ne için | Örnek |
|---|---|---|
| who / that | insan | The woman **who** called you is my aunt. |
| which / that | nesne, hayvan | The phone **which** I bought is broken. |
| where | yer | That's the café **where** we met. |
| whose | aitlik (-in) | The boy **whose** bike was stolen... |
| when | zaman | the day **when** I met her |

- Zamir nesne ise atılabilir: The phone (**that**) I bought...

## B1.G26 Non-defining relative clauses
- Ek bilgi verir, **virgül** arasında: My brother, **who** lives in Izmir, is a doctor.
- Burada **that kullanılmaz** ve zamir atılamaz.

## B1.G27 Dolaylı (kibar) sorular
- **Can you tell me / Do you know / I wonder** + soru kelimesi + **düz cümle**
- Where is the station? → Can you tell me where **the station is**?
- What time does it open? → Do you know what time **it opens**?
- Evet/hayır sorusu → **if**: Do you know **if** the shop is open?

## B1.G29 So do I / Neither do I
- A2.G47 ile aynı kural; B1'de tüm zamanlarla kullanılır:
- I've been to Rome. → **So have I.** / I didn't see it. → **Neither did I.** / I'd love to go. → **So would I.**

## B1.G30 Gerund vs Infinitive
- **+ -ing**: enjoy, avoid, finish, mind, suggest, keep, consider, can't stand, look forward to
- **+ to**: want, decide, hope, plan, agree, refuse, promise, manage, afford, offer
- Özne olarak -ing: **Swimming** is good for you.
- Sıfattan sonra to: It's **easy to learn**.

## B1.G31 stop / remember / try / forget + -ing / to
| Fiil | + -ing | + to |
|---|---|---|
| stop | eylemi bırakmak: I **stopped smoking**. | bir şey yapmak için durmak: I **stopped to smoke**. |
| remember | yapmış olmayı hatırlamak: I remember **locking** the door. | yapmayı unutmamak: Remember **to lock** the door. |
| forget | yaptığını unutmak: I'll never forget **meeting** her. | yapmayı unutmak: I forgot **to call** him. |
| try | denemek (sonuç görmek için): Try **turning** it off. | çabalamak: I tried **to open** it, but I couldn't. |

## B1.G32 to / in order to / so that
- Amaç fiil ise **to / in order to + V1**: I went out **to buy** bread.
- Farklı özne ya da modal gerekiyorsa **so that + cümle**: I saved money **so that** I **could** travel.
- Olumsuz amaç: **so that ... not** / **in order not to**: I left early **so that** I **wouldn't** be late.

## B1.G33 Fiil / sıfat + edat
- **Sıfat**: good **at**, interested **in**, afraid **of**, proud **of**, tired **of**, angry **with**, different **from**, famous **for**, responsible **for**
- **Fiil**: depend **on**, listen **to**, wait **for**, look **at**, agree **with**, belong **to**, worry **about**, apologize **for**
- Edattan sonra fiil **-ing**: I'm interested **in learning** Spanish.

## B1.G34 few / a few / little / a little
- **a few** (sayılabilen) / **a little** (sayılamayan) = biraz, yeterli (olumlu): I have **a few** friends here.
- **few / little** = çok az, yetersiz (olumsuz anlam): I have **few** friends here. (Neredeyse hiç yok.)
- **plenty of** = bolca: We have **plenty of** time.

## B1.G35 both / either / neither
- **both (of)** = ikisi de + çoğul fiil: **Both** of them **are** doctors.
- **either (of)** = ikisinden biri / (olumsuzda) hiçbiri: You can take **either** one.
- **neither (of)** = hiçbiri (olumlu fiille): **Neither** of them came.
- both ... and / either ... or / neither ... nor

## B1.G36 another / other / others / the other
- **another** + tekil = bir başka (ek): Can I have **another** coffee?
- **other** + çoğul = diğer (belirsiz): **Other** people think differently.
- **others** = diğerleri (isimsiz): Some like tea, **others** like coffee.
- **the other** = ikisinden öteki / belirli diğeri: One is red, **the other** is blue.
- **the others** = geri kalanların hepsi.

## B1.G37 -ed / -ing sıfatlar
- **-ed** → kişinin hissi: I'm **bored**. / She's **interested**.
- **-ing** → hissi veren şey: The film is **boring**. / The book is **interesting**.
- ⚠️ I'm boring = Ben sıkıcıyım (başkalarını sıkıyorum)!

## B1.G38 so / such
- **so + sıfat/zarf**: It was **so cold**. / He spoke **so fast**.
- **such (a/an) + (sıfat) + isim**: It was **such a cold day**. / They're **such nice people**.
- Sonuç: so ... **that** / such ... **that**: It was so cold **that** we stayed home.

## B1.G39 Dönüşlü zamirler + each other
| Özne | Dönüşlü |
|---|---|
| I / you | myself / yourself |
| he / she / it | himself / herself / itself |
| we / you / they | ourselves / yourselves / themselves |

- Özne ve nesne aynı kişi: I cut **myself**. / Enjoy **yourselves**!
- by myself = tek başıma. **each other** = birbirini: They love **each other**.

## B1.G40 the ...-er, the ...-er
- İki şey birlikte artar: **The more** you practise, **the better** you get.
- **The sooner, the better.** (Ne kadar erken o kadar iyi.)

## B1.G41 although / despite / however
- **although / even though + cümle**: **Although** it was raining, we went out.
- **despite / in spite of + isim / -ing**: **Despite the rain**, we went out. / Despite **being** tired...
- **however** = ancak, yeni cümle başında virgülle: It was raining. **However**, we went out.

## B1.G42 during / for / while
- **during + isim** (ne zaman): **during** the film
- **for + süre** (ne kadar): **for** two hours
- **while + cümle**: **while** I was eating

## B1.G43 so / therefore / because of / due to / as
- Sebep: **because + cümle** / **because of / due to + isim** / **as / since + cümle** (başta)
- Sonuç: **so** (gündelik), **therefore** (resmî)
- **Because of the traffic**, I was late. = I was late **because** the traffic was bad.

## B1.G44 Sıralama ifadeleri
- **First (of all)**, ... **Then** / **Next**, ... **After that**, ... **Finally**, ...
- **eventually** = sonunda (uzun bir süreçten sonra): He **eventually** found a job.
- **at first** (başta) ≠ **first** (ilk olarak): **At first** I didn't like it, but now I love it.
