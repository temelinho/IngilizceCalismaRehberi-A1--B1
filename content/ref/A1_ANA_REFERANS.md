## 1. a / an (Belirsiz Article)

Karar **yazıya değil okunuşa** göre verilir.

| Okunuşu | Article | Örnek |
|---|---|---|
| Sessiz harf sesiyle başlar | **a** | a company, a book, **a university** (/ju:/ sesi) |
| Sesli harf sesiyle başlar | **an** | an animal, an apple, **an hour** (h okunmaz) |

---

## 2. Çoğul ve -s Takısı Yazımı (-ies)

| Kelime sonu | Kural | Örnek |
|---|---|---|
| **sessiz harf + y** | y düşer → **-ies** | city → cities, study → studies |
| **sesli harf + y** | sadece **-s** | play → plays, boy → boys |
| -s, -sh, -ch, -x, -o | **-es** | wash → washes, fix → fixes, go → goes |

⚠️ Düzeltme: "play → plays" örneği **ies kuralına girmez**; y'den önce sesli harf (a) olduğu için sadece -s alır.

---

## 3. Günler, Aylar ve Zaman Edatları

**Günler:** Monday – Tuesday – **Wednesday** – Thursday – Friday – Saturday – Sunday
⚠️ Yazım: Wednesday (wendesday ❌). Gün ve ay isimleri **büyük harfle** başlar.

**Aylar:** January – February – March – April – May – June – July – August – September – October – November – December

| Edat | Ne ile | Örnek |
|---|---|---|
| **at** | saat | at 7 o'clock, at night |
| **on** | gün / tarih | on Monday, on 5 May |
| **in** | ay, yıl, mevsim, gün parçası | in June, in 2024, in summer, in the evening |

## 4. Geniş Zaman (Present Simple)

Cümle yapısı: **özne + yüklem + nesne**

| | I / you / we / they | he / she / it |
|---|---|---|
| Olumlu | I **work** | He **works** (fiile -s) |
| Olumsuz | I **don't** (do not) work | He **doesn't** (does not) work |
| Soru | **Do** you work? | **Does** he work? |

- he/she/it olumsuz ve soruda **does** gelir ve fiilin **-s takısını kendine çeker**: He doesn't work (works ❌), Does he work? (works ❌)
- **does sadece soru ve olumsuzda** kullanılır.
- am/is/are (to be) ve have/has de geniş zamanda kullanılır.
- **have / has olumsuz:** don't have / doesn't have
  - **have got / has got olumsuz:** haven't got / hasn't got
  - I don't have a car = I haven't got a car.

### Kısa cevaplar
- A: **Do** you work as a computer programmer? B: Yes, I **do**. / No, I **don't**.
- A: **Does** he study at university? B: Yes, he **does**. / No, he **doesn't**.

⚠️ Düzeltme: "Does you he study" → **Does he study**. Yapı: **Do/Does + özne + yalın fiil ...?**

## 5. Sıklık Zarfları

always – usually – often – sometimes – seldom/rarely – never

| Konum | Örnek |
|---|---|
| özne + sıklık zarfı + fiil | I **always** get up early. |
| to be'den **sonra** | She **is always** late. |
| ifadeler (every day, once a week) başta veya sonda | **Every day** I walk. / I walk **every day**. |

---

## 6. Soru Kelimeleri ile Geniş Zaman

Yapı: **Soru kelimesi + do/does + özne + yalın fiil?**

| Soru | Cevap |
|---|---|
| When do you get up? | At 7 am. |
| When does he watch TV? | In the evening. |
| Where do you live? | In Ankara. |
| Where does she work? | She **works** at a factory. |
| What time do you get up? | At 7 am. |
| What time does he watch TV? | He **watches** TV at ten o'clock. |
| What do you buy in the shopping mall? | I buy clothes. |
| What does he play every week? | He plays chess. |
| Who do you live with? | My parents. |
| Who does she meet every day? | She meets her friends every day. |
| Why do you work? | Because I need money. |
| Why does she read books? | To learn new things. |
| How do you go to work? | By bus. |
| How does **he earn** money? | By working hard. |
| How often do you watch TV? | I watch **it** every evening. |
| How often does she eat pizza? | Usually. / Once a week. |

⚠️ Düzeltmeler: "she workd" → **she works**; "How does earns money" → **How does he earn money** (özne eksikti, does varken fiil yalın).

## 7. -s Takısının Okunuşu

| Fiil sonu | Okunuş | Örnek |
|---|---|---|
| Sert ünsüz (f, s, t, k, c, ş, h, p) | /s/ | walks, works, stops |
| Yumuşak ünsüz ve sesli | /z/ | plays (/pleyz/), runs (/ranz/) |
| -es (s, sh, ch, x sonrası) | /ız/ | washes (/woşız/), fixes (/fiksız/) |

---

## 8. Renkler / What color + there is / there are

- Renk sorusu: **What color** is your car? → It's red.
- **there is** + tekil = var: There **is** a cat in the garden. Soru: **Is there** a cat?
- **there are** + çoğul = var: There **are** two cats. Soru: **Are there** any cats?
- **What do you feel like** (doing)? = Canın ne yapmak istiyor?

---

## 9. Şimdiki Zaman (Present Continuous)

Yapı: **am / is / are + fiil-ing**

| Biçim | Örnek |
|---|---|
| Olumlu | I **am working**. |
| Olumsuz | I **am not** working. / You **aren't** reading. / She **isn't** sleeping. |
| Evet/hayır sorusu (be başa) | **Are** you reading a book now? / **Is** she playing tennis? |
| Wh- sorusu (soru kelimesi + be + özne) | Where **are** you studying? / Why **are** they smiling? |

**Gelecek plan anlamı:** Present Continuous, kesinleşmiş yakın gelecek planları için de kullanılır.
- I'm flying to Rome next week.

⚠️ Düzeltme: Notta "-ing eki ile geniş zaman da kullanılır" yazıyordu; doğrusu **şimdiki zamanın gelecek plan anlamı** (geniş zaman değil).

---

## 10. Action Verbs vs State Verbs ⭐ (önemli)

| Tür | -ing alır mı? | Örnekler |
|---|---|---|
| Action (hareket) | ✅ am/is/are + -ing | work, talk, go, sleep, run, listen, read |
| State (durum) | ❌ geniş zaman kalır | want, know, believe, need, love, see, understand |

- I **am reading** now. ✅ / I **know** the answer. ✅ (I am knowing ❌)
- **have**:
  - sahiplik (durum) → -ing almaz: I **have** a car.
  - hareket (yemek, banyo vs.) → -ing alır: I **am having** breakfast.

---

## 11. think (fikir) vs think about (düşünmek)

| Anlam | Yapı | Örnek |
|---|---|---|
| Fikrim bu / bence | think (that) — -ing **almaz** | I **think** (that) the world is small. |
| Bir şeyi düşünmek | think about — -ing **alabilir** | I **am thinking about** my exams now. |

Sorularda da aynı mantık: What do you think? (fikir) / What are you thinking about? (şu an düşündüğün)

---

## 12. would like

Daha **kibar** istemek için: I **would like** some water = I want some water (daha kibar).

- Kısaltma: I'd like
- Fiil gelirse **to** gelir: I would like **to drink** some water. (want to gibi)
- Soru: **Would** + özne + **like**...? → Would you like some water? → Yes, please. / Yes, I would.
- Would you like **to go out** tonight? → No, thanks.

⚠️ **would like = istemek**, **like = sevmek/hoşlanmak**: I like coffee (severim) / I'd like a coffee (bir kahve isterim).

## 13. Özne ve Nesne Zamirleri

| Özne | Nesne | Aitlik |
|---|---|---|
| I | me | my |
| you | you | your |
| he | him | his |
| she | her | her |
| it | it | its |
| we | us | our |
| they | them | their |

Nesne olarak kullanım:
- I am a student → My parents love **me**.
- You are smart → People admire **you**.
- He is great → We trust **him**.
- She lives in a small town → Her friends sometimes visit **her**.

**know** = bilmek / tanımak: I know the answer. / I know him.

---

## 14. a/an vs the (Belirsiz – Belirli Article)

- Belirsiz (ilk kez söz edilen) → **a/an**; belirli (bilinen, daha önce geçen) → **the**
  - Carol is in **a** garden. / Carol is in **the** garden.
  - There is **a** dog in the garden. **The** dog is running after **a** cat. **The** cat is running after **a** mouse. **The** mouse is very fast.
- Tek olan varlıklar **the** alır: the sun, the moon, the sky, the king, the queen, the weather
- **Genellemelerde article yok:** Apples are good for you. / Rice is important for our economy. / Students always do their homework.

## 15. Belgisiz Zamirler

| | some- (olumlu) | any- (olumsuz/soru; olumluda "herhangi") | no- (olumlu cümlede "hiç") |
|---|---|---|---|
| kişi | someone / somebody (birisi) | anyone / anybody | no one / nobody (hiç kimse) |
| şey | something (bir şey) | anything | nothing (hiçbir şey) |
| yer | somewhere (bir yer) | anywhere | nowhere (hiçbir yer) |

- Olumsuzda: I don't know **anyone**. = I know **no one**.
- Olumluda any = herhangi: You can sit **anywhere**.
- ⚠️ no- zaten olumsuz: I know **nothing** ✅ / I don't know nothing ❌

## 16. Geçmiş Zaman (Past Simple)

### to be → was / were
| Özne | was/were |
|---|---|
| I / he / she / it | **was** |
| you / we / they | **were** |

- I am a student → I **was** a student.
- You are beautiful this evening → You **were** beautiful yesterday evening.
- Olumsuz: wasn't / weren't. Soru: **Was** he...? / **Were** you...?

### Normal fiiller
| Biçim | Yapı | Örnek |
|---|---|---|
| Olumlu (düzenli) | fiil + **-ed** | I walked. |
| Olumlu (düzensiz) | **V2** | I slept eight hours last night. |
| Olumsuz | **didn't + yalın fiil** | I didn't walk to school yesterday. |
| Soru | **Did + özne + yalın fiil?** | Did you talk to your teacher yesterday? → Yes, I did. |

⚠️ Hareket (eylem) varsa **did**, durum (sıfat, isim, yer) varsa **was/were**:
- Did you **go** there? / **Were** you tired?

### Düzensiz fiiller (başlangıç)
| V1 | V2 |
|---|---|
| go | went |
| sleep | **slept** |
| buy | bought |
| take | took |
| get | got |
| have | had |
| know | knew |
| speak | spoke |

⚠️ Düzeltme: sleot → **slept**, yesterdeay → yesterday.

## 17. Who / What Soruları (özne mi nesne mi?)

| Ne soruluyor | Yapı | Örnek |
|---|---|---|
| **Özne** (eylemi yapan) | Who + V2 (did YOK) | Mike bought a car last year. → **Who bought** a car last year? |
| **Nesne** (eylemden etkilenen) | Who/What + did + özne + yalın fiil | I met Carol at a cafe. → **Who did you meet** at a cafe? |

- What did you eat last night? → A cake.
- **What vs Which:** what = seçenek belirsiz/açık; which = sınırlı seçenekler arasından.
  - What did you eat? / Which cake did you eat, the chocolate one or the lemon one?
